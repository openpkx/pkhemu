Delete your account

pkhemu has no accounts of its own and runs no servers, so we hold no account or data to delete.

RetroAchievements

pkhemu's "Create an account" button opens the sign-up page of RetroAchievements, a separate service. RetroAchievements keeps that account and your unlocks, not us.

  • To remove it from pkhemu, open Settings, then RetroAchievements, and tap Sign out. This deletes the sign-in token from your device.
  • To delete the account and its data, sign in at retroachievements.org/settings and request deletion there. RetroAchievements deletes the account after 14 days unless you cancel. Only you can request it.

Sync with Google Drive or Dropbox

Sync uses your own Google or Dropbox account. Disconnect in Settings to delete the sign-in token from your device, then delete the files as the privacy policy explains.
