pkhemu is an emulator with a save editor built in

pkhemu plays GB, GBC, GBA and DS games. For supported games, pause and edit your save, then keep playing.

Coming soon to Android and macOS

What it does

  • Bring your own games. pkhemu ships no games or BIOS files. Add the games you own from a folder on your device.
  • Edit saves. For supported games, change your save while the game is paused, and move characters and items between your saves.
  • Save states and backups. Save anywhere, and go back to an earlier copy of your save.
  • Open source. pkhemu is licensed under the GPL-3.0. The source code is published with the first release.

Your data

pkhemu runs no servers and has no accounts, ads or analytics. Your games and saves stay on your device.

You can connect your Google Drive or Dropbox to copy your saves and save states between your own devices. pkhemu then asks Google for two things: your email address, to show which account is connected, and access to a private app folder in your Drive that only pkhemu can see. It can't see any other file in your Drive. The privacy policy has the details.

Built on

PKHeX for save editing, and mGBA and melonDS for emulation. Consider supporting them.
