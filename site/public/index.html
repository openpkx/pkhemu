What it does

  • Bring your own games

    pkhemu ships no games or BIOS files. Add the games you own from a folder.

  • Edit saves

    Change your save while the game is paused, and move characters and items between saves.

  • Save states and backups

    Save anywhere, and go back to an earlier copy of your save.

  • Open source

    Licensed under the GPL-3.0. The source of each release is on GitHub.

Your data

No servers, accounts, ads or analytics. Your games and saves stay on your device.

You can connect your Google Drive or Dropbox to copy your saves and save states between your own devices. pkhemu then asks Google for two things: your email address, to show which account is connected, and access to a private app folder in your Drive that only pkhemu can see. It can't see any other file in your Drive. The privacy policy has the details.

Built on PKHeX for save editing, and mGBA, melonDS, Mupen64Plus-Next and Azahar for emulation. Consider supporting them.
