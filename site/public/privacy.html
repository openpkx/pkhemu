Privacy policy

Updated 16 October 2026

pkhemu is an emulator app for Android, iPhone, iPad and macOS. We run no servers and collect no personal data. The app has no accounts of its own, and no ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Your games, saves, save states, backups, settings and play history stay on your device. We never receive them.

Sync with Google Drive or Dropbox

Sync is optional. If you connect Google Drive or Dropbox, pkhemu uploads your saves, save states, backups and kept copies to your own account. If you turn on Sync games, it uploads your game files too. The files go straight from your device to Google or Dropbox, never through us.

  • On Google Drive, pkhemu uses the hidden app data folder. Other apps can't see it, and pkhemu can't see the rest of your Drive.
  • On Dropbox, pkhemu uses the Apps/pkhemu folder and can't see the rest of your Dropbox.
  • pkhemu reads your account's email address only to show which account is connected.
  • Sign-in tokens stay on your device.

To stop syncing, disconnect in the app's Settings. You can also remove pkhemu's access from your Google account or your Dropbox account. Files already uploaded stay in your cloud storage until you delete them. On Google Drive, delete them under Settings, Manage apps, pkhemu, Delete hidden app data. On Dropbox, delete the Apps/pkhemu folder.

pkhemu's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google user data

This section covers the data pkhemu gets from Google when you connect Google Drive.

  • What pkhemu accesses. Your Google account's email address, through the email and openid scopes. The files pkhemu itself creates in your Drive's hidden app data folder, through the drive.appdata scope. pkhemu can't read, list or change any other file in your Drive.
  • How pkhemu uses it. The email address is shown in Settings so you know which account is connected. The app data folder holds copies of your saves, save states, backups and kept copies, and your game files if you turn on Sync games, so your other devices can download them.
  • Where it's stored. The files stay in your own Google Drive. The sign-in token and the email address are stored only on your device, in the system keychain or the app's private storage.
  • Sharing. pkhemu doesn't send Google user data to us or to anyone else. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI or machine learning models.
  • Keeping and deleting. The files stay in your Drive until you delete them. Disconnecting in Settings deletes the sign-in token from your device. To delete the files, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings, Manage apps, pkhemu, and choose Delete hidden app data.

Box art

pkhemu downloads cover images for your games from the libretro-thumbnails project on GitHub. Each request includes the game's name, and GitHub sees your IP address, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Update check

The iOS build you install with SideStore or AltStore checks for a newer version when you open it, at most once a day. It downloads a short file from GitHub that names the latest version, and GitHub sees your IP address, under GitHub's privacy statement.

RetroAchievements

RetroAchievements is optional. If you sign in to your retroachievements.org account in Settings, pkhemu sends your username and password to RetroAchievements to sign in. It keeps only the sign-in token RetroAchievements returns, on your device, and never stores your password. While you play, pkhemu sends RetroAchievements which game you're playing, the achievements you unlock, your leaderboard scores and a short line about where you are in the game. This goes straight from your device to RetroAchievements, never through us, under RetroAchievements' privacy policy. Signing out in Settings deletes the token from your device. To delete what RetroAchievements keeps, use your account settings on retroachievements.org.

Purchases

If you buy something in the Google Play version, Google Play handles the payment under Google's privacy policy. We don't receive your payment details. The app asks Google Play on your device which purchases you own.

Children

We collect no personal data from anyone, including children.
